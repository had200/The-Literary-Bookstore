## ✦ About the Project

**The Literary Bookstore** is a mobile application developed using **Flutter**, **Dart**, **PHP**, and **MySQL**.

Designed for book lovers, it offers a simple and enjoyable way to discover books, explore categories, search for titles, and enjoy a personalized shopping experience.

> *"A reader lives a thousand lives before he dies. The man who never reads lives only one."*
> — **George R.R. Martin**

---

## ✦ Features

| ✦ | Feature | Description |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| 📖 | **Browse & Discover** | Wander through a curated collection of books. |
| 🔍 | **Search & Explore** | Find titles, authors, and categories in seconds. |
| 📚 | **Detailed Book Views** | Dive deep into synopses, covers, and details. |
| ♥ | **Favorites** | Keep your beloved books close to your heart. |
| 🛒 | **Shopping Cart** | Collect your next reading adventures. |
| 🌙 | **Light & Dark Themes** | Read by daylight or by candlelight. |
| 👤 | **Personal Profile** | Your own corner of the bookstore. |
| ⚙️ | **Admin Dashboard** | Manage books and users with elegance. |
